Research projects

Completed projects.

2025

Motivation vs. personality: what drives belonging for international students?

Supervised by Dr. Faith Dillon-Lee · University of Edinburgh

Investigated how motivation to engage and personality (e.g. extraversion) shape international students' sense of belonging, using a Qualtrics survey and mixed methods (thematic analysis + mediation analysis). Findings suggest extraversion, not motivation, significantly predicts belongingness. Presented at The EAP Student: Identity, Community, and Belonging Conference, Edinburgh.

Presented
2024 — 25

Direct vs. indirect quotation in written sarcasm comprehension

With Joshua Hew · Supervised by Prof. Martin Corley

Pre-registered self-paced reading study (PCIbex) on whether sarcasm is easier to recover from direct than reported speech. Designed and implemented an online experiment using PCIbex (JavaScript), created stimuli, prepared materials, analysed data, with write-up in plan. Initial results suggest readers comprehend sarcasm faster in direct quotations, though the effect is not statistically significant. 

Writing up
2022 — 23

Real-life café vs. virtual café noise effects on creative work performance

Supervised by Dr. Chris Egan · funded by ESRA (£100)

Compared creative-work performance between participants working in actual coffee shops or libraries and participants listening to virtual café / library white noise. Designed and implemented the study independently using Qualtrics and the Remote Associates Test; analysed data from 30 participants. Real-environment groups achieved significantly higher creative scores than virtual-noise groups. Presented as a poster at the ESRA Annual Conference.
